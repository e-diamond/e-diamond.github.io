One of the best educational videos I've seen for a while. Chris deep-dives into the (surprisingly short) history of <span lang="ko">지산법</span> (romanised _chisanbop_), a method of finger counting developed by Hang Young Pai, and its attempt to be introduced as a tool for maths education in schools in the US. It is long, but deserves every second of it's 1 hour 23 minute runtime. One thing not mentioned in the video which I think is of interest is the origin of the name chisanbop. It is made up of the Chinese characters <strong lang="ko">指算法</strong>, which mean **finger**, **counting**, and **method** respectively.
